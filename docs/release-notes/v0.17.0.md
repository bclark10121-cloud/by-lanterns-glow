# By Lantern's Glow v0.17.0

The lantern release. Eight new treasures that show only inside your lantern light after dark, a storybook talk box and a new Lantern Hill title card.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.16.0. Your saves stay.
2. Open the game. The title card is a painted night sky: stars, a crescent moon, rolling hills and one lantern glowing on a hill, with fireflies. The name glows softly. Continue, Play, Settings and Version 0.17.0 sit below.
3. Continue your game and walk up to any neighbor. Press A. A parchment box opens with their name on a red ribbon. The words write themselves out with soft voice blips. Press A once to finish the page. Press again for the next page or to close.
4. Open Menu, then Settings. Under By Lantern's Glow, find Voice blips. Turn it off and talk to a neighbor again. The text still writes out, now silent.
5. Wait for night with the Lantern (Tester tools: Time of day, or Day and night). Walk north of the meadow pond. A soft gold glow and a faint twinkle show near your lantern. Walk closer. The Moonmoss Stone appears. Pick it up.
6. Open Menu, Journal, Special Finds. Lantern Lights shows 1 of 8.

## New in this version

- Lantern Lights, a new set of 8 treasures in the Special Finds page of the journal. They show only after dark, only with the Lantern and only inside its light. Outside the light, a faint twinkle gives them away. One sits in each corner of the Hollow: the meadow, the shore, the northern woods, the Autumn Woods (two), the Snowy Peaks (two) and Sunny Canyon. The museum has a new case for the set. 156 treasures in all.
- The first night you carry the Lantern near one, a tip tells you to look inside its light.
- A storybook talk box for every message. Parchment, the speaker's name on a red ribbon, text that writes itself out and long talks split into pages. A finishes a page, then turns it, then closes. Tap the box or the world to do the same.
- Voice blips: each neighbor has a voice pitch of their own. Settings, Voice blips turns them off. Sound at Off silences them too. Reduce motion shows each page at once.
- The Lantern Hill title card, drawn in code: night sky, crescent moon, hills, far village windows, a lantern on the near hill and fireflies. The title glows. Reduce motion holds it still. On a wide screen the lantern stands to the left of the buttons.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.9.0 (the newest kit for plain HTML games).

## Install

Version 0.17.0 installs over 0.16.0 and keeps your saves. It installs next to the old Helena Hollow app and does not replace it.

## Full test checklist

1. Title card: the sky, moon, hill lantern and fireflies draw on a phone held upright, a phone held sideways and a desktop. Buttons stay readable.
2. Reduce motion on: the title card stops moving and the talk box shows each page at once.
3. Talk box: neighbor name on the ribbon, signs and notes without a ribbon, pages turn with A, tap or Enter, walking away closes it.
4. Voice blips on and off. Sound at Off silences the blips.
5. Lantern Lights: hidden in daylight, hidden without the Lantern, visible at night only near the lantern, counted in the journal and the museum.
6. With Day and night off, Lantern Lights still show inside the lantern light.

## Tested and not tested

Tested in headless Chrome on the cloud test machine: a new suite for this release (t36, 52 checks on a 960 x 540 screen and a phone held upright), the standards suite (t35), the menu fit suite at 12 screen sizes (t22), the walk test that reaches every outdoor treasure (t27), the village, museum, quiet, comfort and progress suites and the quick check with the pretend Xbox controller. Screenshots of each new screen were checked by eye. The signed APK was checked for its signature, the tester gate and no internet permission.

Not tested: a real phone, the Retroid or a real Xbox controller. Voice blips were not heard by ear.
