# By Lantern's Glow v0.17.1

A start screen of its own. Lantern Hill stays with Pixel Hollow (BC, 2026-11-17).

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.17.0. Your saves stay.
2. Open the game. The start screen is dawn in the peaks: snowcapped mountains above a sea of pink clouds, the sun rising, birds crossing, a wildflower meadow in front and a lantern swinging on a trail signpost.
3. The controls line stays readable over the bright clouds. Continue, Play and Settings work as in v0.17.0.
4. Turn on Reduce motion. The clouds, flowers and lantern hold still.

## New in this version

- The Dawn Peaks start screen, drawn in code, in place of Lantern Hill.
- A stronger shadow on the controls line.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.9.0.

## Install

Version 0.17.1 installs over 0.17.0 and keeps your saves.

## Tested and not tested

Tested in headless Chrome on the cloud test machine: the v0.17 suite (t36, talk box, start screen and Lantern Lights), the standards suite (t35), the menu fit suite at 12 screen sizes (t22) and the quick check with the pretend Xbox controller. All passed. Screenshots at four screen sizes were checked by eye. The signed APK was checked for its signature, the tester gate and no internet permission.

Not tested: a real phone, the Retroid or a real Xbox controller.
